Level two asks why. Among everyone whose deadline has passed, the tool compares groups. People literate in Latin script reach A1 on time in 62 percent of cases, the others in 35. Schooling, motivation and psychological burden matter too – and those last two come from the free-text Situationsanalyse, read by AI. Two rules we hold to: groups smaller than ten people are never shown, and there is never a score for an individual. And we label these as associations, not causes – for example, people in literacy courses do worse, simply because those courses go to the people who need them most.
