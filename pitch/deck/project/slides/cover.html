AI for Access Hackathon · Bern 2026

Integrations-Cockpit

From Excel exports to “who do I call this week, and why?”

Challenge by Stadt Bern, Asylsozialdienst · Team IntegrationCockpit

Hi, we are team IntegrationCockpit. We took the challenge of the Asylsozialdienst of the City of Bern. In one sentence: we turn the Excel exports they already have into a cockpit that tells every social worker who to call this week, and why – and tells management where the service stands against its integration goals.
