What it found in the demo data

24 people proved A1 on time – but the goal was never recorded as reached

With success-based funding, each of them is a payment to check.

6 jobs reported to the federal ZEMIS register but missing in the ASD’s own employment list

3 valid A1 certificates without a date – they cannot count

3 clients without an entry date – no deadline can be computed

This is where it gets concrete. In the demo data the tool found 24 people who proved their A1 level in time – but the goal was never recorded as reached. Since the ASD is paid per goal reached, every one of them is a payment to check. It also compares the federal ZEMIS employment register with the ASD’s own employment list and flags jobs that were never entered, and it lists records that make a client impossible to assess. The data-quality sheet is the to-do list.
